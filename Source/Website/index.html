One mug. Four useful habits.

The things you reach for all day, made easier to reach.

⌃⌥⌘

Caps Lock earns its place

Hold Caps Lock for Meh (Control, Option, Command), or choose Hyper to add Shift. A quick tap sends Escape. Your shortcuts stay yours.

☕

Keep the screen awake

Click the menu bar mug to toggle Caffeine for this session. Its filled or empty state tells you where you stand; the next launch uses your saved preference.

↖

Give buttons a job

Map Back, Forward, and the Logitech MX Master 3S thumb button to a shortcut or shell command. Invert mouse wheel scrolling when you want to.

↔

Swipe where you mean to go

Assign three- and four-finger swipes in any direction. A one-time macOS setup disables conflicting system gestures before you use them.

Quiet in the menu bar. Clear in a file.

Left-click the mug to switch Caffeine. Right-click for Settings, privilege checks, trackpad setup, and update checks. Preferences live in ~/.config/hypermug.json, with a generated schema for editor autocomplete.

Make room for HyperMug.

The Homebrew cask becomes available after the first compiled production release and its cask update is merged. Source-only releases can be built locally. For a compiled release, install the cask and grant Accessibility and Input Monitoring when prompted. Input Monitoring takes effect after a relaunch.
